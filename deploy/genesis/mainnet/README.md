# Mainnet genesis inputs

Public inputs for `genesis-ceremony`. The keys are not here: each validator key and
council key is generated by its holder on their own machine
(`docs/runbooks/regenesis-checklist.md` §2), and neither Claude session holds one.

## `mining.json` (ADR-044, ADR-068, DECISIONS 2026-10-29)

| Field | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `initial_epoch_reward` | 299452429842573579739904175 | about 299.45 coins an epoch; era 1 pays about 10.5M coins. The four halving eras pay 19,687,500 coins in total (ADR-068), not 21M |
| `halving_epochs` | 35064 | about 4 years at one block a second and 3,600-block epochs |
| `tail_epoch_reward` | 23956194387405886379192334 | about 23.96 coins an epoch, forever. Annual tail is 210,000 coins at one block a second, 105,000 at two (see below) |

Facts, computed from `MiningRecord` (`crates/node/src/state/mining.rs`), not from the 21M figure:

- The four halving eras pay **19,687,500 coins** in total (exactly 19687499999999999999999999910864
  base units after per-halving truncation). There is no protocol cap: after that, the tail pays
  forever.
- The tail starts at **epoch 140,256** (era 4, where the halved reward 18.72 coins falls under
  the tail). At one block a second that is about 16.0 years; at two seconds, about 32 years.
- Tail rate accepted by the owner (DECISIONS 2026-11-07): about 1.07% a year at one block a
  second. The rate follows block speed, because DAG-BFT does not pin block time: the tail is
  per epoch, so issuance per year is 23.96 × (seconds per year ÷ (3,600 × seconds per block)),
  which is 210,000 coins a year at 1 s per block and 105,000 at 2 s. Relative to the 19.69M
  halving-era issuance at tail start: about 1.07% at 1 s, 0.53% at 2 s. It falls every year
  after that. It can be retuned later by a protocol upgrade at an activation height; genesis
  does not fix it.
  The measured devnet rate is 0.96-1.03 blocks a second (`reports/18-economics.md`); the 2.6 s
  per block in `reports/bench/2026-10-25-tps-series.md` was under load.
| `miner_share_bps` | 7000 | 70% to miners, 30% to the committee |
| `target_shares` / `max_shares_per_block` | 1000 / 16 | 16 × 3,600 ≥ 4 × 1,000, so the ×4 retarget can harden fully |
| `initial_target` | `0000ff…` | an opening share target; the per-epoch retarget (at most ×4) finds the real rate |
| `dag` | `mainnet` | the 4 GiB dataset |

If the mainnet block rate is not about one block a second, recompute `halving_epochs` and
`tail_epoch_reward` before signing.

## The reserve

Rewards are never minted. They are paid from a keyless reserve that genesis funds with the
schedule's exact lifetime emission (`MiningRecord::lifetime_emission`, to the last `u64`
height):

```
--mining-reserve 122753829695359067419575633036805965021648
```

That is about 1.23 × 10^17 coins (about 2^136.5 base units), under the 2^192 ceiling. The tool
refuses any less.

## The command (run on genesis day, after the keys exist)

```
genesis-ceremony --chain-id maya-mainnet \
  --supply 0 \
  --validators validators.txt \
  --mining deploy/genesis/mainnet/mining.json \
  --mining-reserve 122753829695359067419575633036805965021648 \
  --council council-public --council-threshold 2 --council-max-pause-blocks <N> \
  --min-self-bond <UNITS> --min-delegation <UNITS> \
  --timestamp <UNIX> --out-dir out
cargo xtask genesis-check out/genesis.json
```

- `--supply 0` is the only mainnet path (ADR-044, Eric 2026-11-07): no premine; genesis holds
  only the keyless mining reserve and the disclosed minimum validator bonds (7 x
  min-self-bond = 7 × 1,000 = 7,000 coins, about 0.036% of the 19.69M era issuance; DECISIONS
  2026-11-08). The bonds are minted value, which is why they are disclosed. The ceremony
  refuses any other supply on `maya-mainnet`, and writes no root key and no treasury.
  `genesis-check` refuses any allocation that is not the reserve, and a reserve that is not
  exactly the schedule's `lifetime_emission`.
- `validators.txt`: one line per validator, `label key operator bond`, with at least 7
  validators and no operator above 20% of the bonded stake. Every bond is exactly
  `--min-self-bond` (1,000 coins, DECISIONS 2026-11-08); the ceremony and `genesis-check` both
  refuse any other amount. Stake concentration is therefore the seat count, which the 20% cap
  already bounds.
- The check counts payout addresses. At launch every validator is run by one person
  (DECISIONS 2026-10-25), so each gets its own operator address. The launch says so publicly:
  "validators run by one operator", never "independent".
- For `maya-mainnet` the tool adds what the chain needs on its own:
  - stake-weighted voting (ADR-040);
  - a refusal to run without the council (ADR-050);
  - the fee market with `min_base_fee` = 10^6 (S-007).
- `--min-self-bond` and `--min-delegation` are required on `maya-mainnet`. The devnet
  defaults are 10^-21 and 10^-23 of a coin. Mainnet: 1,000 coins
  (`1000000000000000000000000000`) and 1 coin (`1000000000000000000000000`), decided
  2026-10-30 (DECISIONS.md).
- `genesis-check` refuses a file that breaks any of these rules (`xtask/src/genesis_check.rs`,
  `problems` and `launch_rules`):
  - **No premine:** every allocation is the keyless mining reserve, and nothing else is funded.
  - **No pending upgrade:** `protocol_upgrades` is empty.
  - **Clock:** the timestamp is at most 365 days old and at most 90 days ahead of the check.
  - **Tail:** `bft.mining.tail_epoch_reward` is above zero.
  - **Reserve:** its balance is exactly `MiningRecord::lifetime_emission` (ADR-044).
  - **Validators:** at least 7, and one bond per validator.
  - **Anchor timeout:** `bft.anchor_timeout_ms` is at least 500.
  - **Staking floors:** `min_self_bond` is set and at least 1,000 coins; `min_delegation` is set
    and at least 1 coin.
  - **Bonds:** every bond equals `min_self_bond` exactly.
  - **Operator cap:** no operator holds more than 20% of the bonded stake.
  - **Shielded pool:** off (`shielded_activation_height` = `u64::MAX`, ADR-037).
  - **Voting:** stake-weighted.
  - **Proof of work:** `difficulty_bits` is non-zero, or `bft.mining` is present.
  - **Fees:** `bft.fees` is present and `min_base_fee` is at least 10^6 (S-007).
  - **Stake:** total bonded stake is above zero.
  - Not checked by `genesis-check`: `suite_deprecations` (the field is not read). Check it by
    hand: it must be empty.
- Pre-signing, in addition to `genesis-check`: `maya-mainnet` is registered in
  `Network::for_chain_id` (`crates/address/src/lib.rs`), so mainnet addresses have a text form.
  `cargo test -p maya-address` enforces it.

## Dry run (2026-10-30, throwaway keys, nothing published)

Inputs: 7 throwaway validator keys, 3 throwaway council keys, and the values above. Results:

- The ceremony wrote the genesis, with total supply 122753829695429068419575633036805965021648 units.
- `cargo xtask genesis-check`: **PASS**. Fees were 1000000/1000000, stake-weighted, council 2-of-3. This ran before the 2026-11-07 decisions (`--supply 0`, every bond equal to `min_self_bond`). Re-run `genesis-check` on the real candidate file before signing; this PASS does not carry over.
- `maya2c-node` booted from it as `validator 0 of 7` on `maya-mainnet`, with genesis id `28210c54…`. That id is not mainnet's: the real keys and timestamp change it.
- Rejoin guard (B49): a node restarted later with no confirming head, and a tip older than the unbonding window, is refused unless it has `--trusted-block` or `--bootstrap-from`. `--allow-unconfirmed-start` is for a network halted longer than the window or a genesis signed long before launch only. Do not use it to get past a refusal without knowing which case applies. Runbook: `docs/runbooks/hybrid-testnet-hardening.md` section 4.

Signing the real genesis is **APPROVED: sign genesis**, a separate approval from the re-genesis.

## Bootnode peer id (B27)

The mainnet bootnode's peer id is `MAINNET_BOOTNODE_PEER_ID` in [`bootnode.env`](bootnode.env).
It is `SET-AT-GENESIS` until the ceremony creates the bootnode key. Every mainnet join path
must take the id from that file. The testnet guides name the testnet bootnode id, which is
a different chain's id. The mainnet join script does not exist yet.
