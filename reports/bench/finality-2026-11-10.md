# bench-finality (localnet, 4 validators on one machine)

- commit: 027c7813
- os: windows / Microsoft Windows [Version 10.0.29680.1000]
- hardware: Intel64 Family 6 Model 198 Stepping 2, GenuineIntel; 24 logical cores; RAM MISSING
- command: cargo xtask bench-finality --rate 10
- mix: single-output transfers of 1 base unit, 30 senders x 10 nonces to one recipient, hybrid ML-DSA-65 + SLH-DSA
- offered rate: 10.0 tx/s open loop; achieved 10.0 accepted/s over 29.98s
- posted: 300; accepted by a mempool: 300; included: 300; finalized: 300

Latency in milliseconds, nearest-rank percentiles, from the moment the post is sent:

| clock | n | p50 | p90 | p99 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| submit to included | 300 | 4971.7 | 10371.2 | 12771.7 | 14223.3 |
| submit to finalized | 300 | 5014.3 | 10710.1 | 12810.5 | 14320.7 |

- included: node 3 serves a block carrying the txid (polled every 10 ms, so +/- 10 ms).
- finalized: node 3's `get_checkpoint` is a quorum-attested block (2f+1, ADR-038) at or above the including block.

Single machine, localhost links, other processes were running: no network latency is in these figures, and a real network will be slower. Signing excluded. Transactions not finalized are counted above, not hidden.
Not measured: WAN, separate machines, contract calls, mixed loads, rates other than the one stated.
