## What changed and why

- **governance threshold harnesses.** Symbolic weights were `u128`, then `u64`,
  and CBMC gave no verdict. The hard part is the comparison `e*a <= e*K` between
  two symbolic-by-symbolic multipliers, where `a` is the approval threshold.
  Single-limb weights (`u8`) close in about a minute. Weights of 16 bits and
  wider did not. **So these two harnesses prove the threshold logic only for
  weights below 2^8.** The multi-limb behaviour (weights of 2^64 and up) is
  checked by a deterministic sweep, `crates/governance/tests/threshold_extremes_tests.rs`
  (2 tests, pass in 6.8 s). It is evidence, not a proof.
- **fee-market.** Bounded the inputs that shrink the circuits: the treasury share
  is a `u16` (the clamp at `BPS` is still covered), and size, target and
  denominator are `u32` widened to `u64`. The 256-bit fee and floor stay full
  width. These did not get a verdict in budget, so they are moved to / kept in
  kani-heavy.yml with notes. The bound on the base-fee harnesses is documented
  as a bound the proof is not yet verified under.
- **dex.** A 41-bit mask on the inputs was tried. It did not remove
  `Wide::long_div` from the model, so the rationale I first wrote was wrong. The
  run died with no verdict, and the proof is reverted to HEAD. The dex heavy
  entries are unchanged.
- **Workflows.** nightly.yml: governance removed from the heavy list (its two
  harnesses now run in nightly), fee-market heavy list = the three division-bound
  harnesses. kani-heavy.yml: governance matrix entries removed; fee-market
  entries annotated with the measured results; stale `unwind(513)` comment
  removed. The name-exists and count guards are unchanged.
