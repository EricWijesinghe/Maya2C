## What I found

1. **Fixed: a consensus safety bug.**
   - The voting threshold was safe only at 4, 7, 10… validators.
   - At 6 validators the network could have split into two halves that
     each finalised different history.
   - Fixed (ADR-039), with tests that prove the halves now stop instead of
     splitting.
   - No impact on the live testnet: it runs at a safe size.
2. **Open: anyone can stop the chain cheaply.**
   - Every validator seat is one vote, and a seat costs 1,000 test coins.
     The faucet gives away 200,000.
   - Seats whose node never comes online stop the chain, and it cannot
     recover by itself.
   - What I did:
     - **today's testnet is protected**: our validator refuses cheap
       registrations;
     - I built the real fix into the engine, so votes count by stake
       (ADR-040, PR #59, not merged yet).
   - This is now **mainnet gate 10**.
