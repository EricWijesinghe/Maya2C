## Why the first start was slow

Headers arrive 2,000 per request, which is fast. Block bodies above the
snapshot (3,600–7,200) and the catch-up blocks came **one request at a time**,
at about 0.35 s each through `bootstrap.maya2c.dev`. The gateway limits each
client to 10/s (`--client-ip cf-connecting-ip`), so the rate limit was not
the cause; the round trips were. `state_pruner::prefetch` now keeps 4 requests
in flight and still links and validates every block in order.
