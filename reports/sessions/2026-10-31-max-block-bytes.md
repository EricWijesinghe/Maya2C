- `crates/fee-market/src/limits.rs`: `MAX_BLOCK_BYTES` added as a separate item (additive, after `MAX_TARGET_BLOCK_BYTES`).
- `crates/node/src/core/block.rs`: `block_wire_len`, `transaction_wire_len`, `admitted_prefix`, `Block::check_size`; compile-time assert that the cap fits a maximal transaction.

## Chosen value: 5 MiB (5,242,880 B), provisional, pending measurement on the launch host

Bounds:
- Lower: `MAX_TX_BYTES` (4 MiB) plus framing (161 B) must fit alone. Otherwise a valid transaction could never be included and would block the queue behind it. Compile-time assert in `core/block.rs`.
- Upper: a block at the cap should fit one 8 MiB gossip frame (`network/behaviour.rs` `MAX_GOSSIP_MESSAGE_BYTES`) with envelope room. Block sync allows 24 MiB payload (`network/sync.rs`).
- Measured: `reports/12-baseline.md` apply of one hybrid transfer, one thread, optimized, incl. verify = 1.062 ms; hybrid tx 11,165 B (`docs/blockgraph.md`). Derived: about 100 ms/MiB serial, about 37 ms/MiB with 4-thread verify (0.324 + 0.071 ms per tx). 5 MiB gives about 0.2 to 0.5 s apply.
- Measured block: `reports/bench/2026-10-25-tps-series.md`, 400 transfers per block took about 2.6 s on a shared machine. 400 x 11,165 B is about 4.3 MiB, so 5 MiB admits it.

Apply cost figures are **derived from the 2026-09-26 record, not re-measured.** A measurement of apply time per MiB on the launch host is MISSING.

Gaps and conflicts:
- `MAX_TARGET_BLOCK_BYTES` is 16 MiB. A fee target above 5 MiB cannot be reached. Needs its own decision (ADR-072 Consequences).
- The 400-transfer throughput figure (153 tx/s) was measured above this cap. It needs re-measuring under the cap; not claimed here.
- Invariant 33 and ADR-072 may collide with numbers in the #274 branch (`origin/fix/base-fee-clamp` has ADR-071 and invariant 32 only, so 33 and 072 look free).
