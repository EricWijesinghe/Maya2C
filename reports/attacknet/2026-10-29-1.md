## Diagnosis (from the node logs in target/attacknet)

- Attack 8 drove the S-009 path as intended: validator 4, restarted with
  `--catch-up-from` validator 3 (behind it), logged "certificate refused: the
  peer is behind this node; falling back to checkpoints".
- What failed was the checkpoint path behind it. When attack 8 began, validator
  6 was still an attested follower ("observer of 7 validators") from the
  long-outage attack, and attack 8 took two more validators down. Three nodes
  then needed checkpoints, more than f = 2. The checkpoint at height 180 carried
  3 signatures, short of the n − f quorum the followers count. Validator 3
  exited ("short of a quorum"), and validators 4 and 6 retried without
  progress, while validators 0, 1, 2 and 5 kept building.
- This is the open gate-10 case: more than f nodes that each need to follow.
  The checkpoint threshold stays n − f (Eric, 2026-10-05). A fix needs a
  design decision (p2p block fetch cross-checked by n − f, per the earlier
  note), so this report records it and does not work around it.
- Still unexplained: why a 7-seat epoch's checkpoint formed with 3
  signatures at all. The server's collector should refuse one below its own
  quorum, so the server and follower may disagree on epoch 3's committee
  (jailing after downtime). Recorded as pre-audit finding S-011.
