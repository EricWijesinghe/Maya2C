Before the first start

What mining does to this computer while it runs.

  • Heat and noise. The devices you pick run at full load, get hot, and their fans speed up.
  • Power. They draw power the whole time, on your electricity.
  • The 80 °C stop. The Hub stops a GPU above it and restarts it once it is 5 °C cooler. Change it in Settings.
  • Testnet MAYA has no value. It tests the network; nothing here is income.

Your payout key

Rewards go to the account in this file. The Hub creates it on the first start. Whoever holds the file holds the account, and nobody can restore it for you.
