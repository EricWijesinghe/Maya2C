# Prior art: protocol-level revenue share for contract developers

**Search status:** not searched (from knowledge, 2026-09-26).

**Who else does something similar.** These are all from knowledge and
unverified:
- NEAR sends a share of gas fees from calls to a contract's account;
- Canto proposed Contract Secured Revenue (CSR);
- Archway has developer rewards;
- several Ethereum L2s run sequencer fee-sharing programmes.

**How Maya2C differs.** Nothing is built. The fee market is inactive, so
there is no fee to share.

**What may be said publicly.** Nothing.
