# Prior art: Native smart accounts as the only account type

**Search status:** partially searched 2026-09-26.

**Who else does something similar.** Ethereum made EOAs programmable with EIP-7702 on 7 May 2025 ([EF](https://blog.ethereum.org/2025/04/23/pectra-mainnet)) on top of ERC-4337. From knowledge, **not searched**: Starknet and zkSync Era have native account abstraction.

**How Maya2C differs.** The difference Maya2C aims at is post-quantum keys underneath native accounts. `crates/smart-account` exists but is not the default account type.
