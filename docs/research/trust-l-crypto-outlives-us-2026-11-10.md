## Prior-art notes (searched 2026-11-10; abstracts and secondary coverage flagged)

- Standards: FIPS 203/204/205 final; FIPS 206 (FN-DSA) submitted 2025-08-28, not final (vendor sources); draft-ietf-lamps-fn-dsa-certificates (https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-lamps-fn-dsa-certificates/); HQC / FIPS 207 selected 2025, no published draft (https://csrc.nist.gov/presentations/2025/fips-207-hqc-kem); onramp round 3 (https://groups.google.com/a/list.nist.gov/g/pqc-forum/c/LXoTAe5AN78); NIST IR 8547 final listed Oct 2026 (https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8547/final) — deprecate quantum-vulnerable after 2030, disallow after 2035; CNSA 2.0 dates vendor-only, sources disagree 2033 vs 2035.
- Threat estimates (estimates, not attacks): Gidney 2025 RSA-2048 < 1M noisy qubits (https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15917); Google Quantum AI ECDLP-256 ~1,200–1,450 logical qubits (https://research.google/pubs/securing-elliptic-curve-cryptocurrencies-against-quantum-vulnerabilities-resource-estimates-and-mitigations-2/); no estimate found breaking ML-KEM/ML-DSA; Chen ePrint 2024/555 quantum LWE claim withdrawn after a bug; GRI 2025 survey 28–49% CRQC within 10 years (https://globalriskinstitute.org/publication/quantum-threat-timeline-report-2025b/).
- Canary: Todd bounties (https://mirror.b10c.me/lists/bitcoindev/20130913060758.GC4242@savin/T, https://mirror.b10c.me/lists/bitcoindev/20170225205706.GA16059@savin.petertodd.org) — SHA1 paid after public disclosure (confirmation, not early warning); BitMEX canary fund (https://invezz.com/news/2026/04/16/bitmex-floats-wait-and-see-alternative-to-bip-361-quantum-freeze/) triggers a freeze, Maya2C's would schedule a sunset; Q-Day Prize (https://blog.projecteleven.com/posts/project-eleven-awards-1-btc-q-day-prize-for-largest-quantum-attack-on-elliptic-curve-cryptography-to-date) contested, sponsor conflict → checkers must be mechanical and on chain.
- Migration: BIP-361 (https://bips.dev/361/); Buterin 2024 (https://ethresear.ch/t/how-to-hard-fork-to-save-most-users-funds-in-a-quantum-emergency/18901) — its rollback conflicts with the no-rewind position, adopt only the STARK seed proof; Algorand Falcon (https://algorand.co/technology/post-quantum) — do not repeat their "first" wording.
- Hybrid/KEM/archival: X-Wing -11 (https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-connolly-cfrg-xwing-kem-11.html), proof (https://pub.sandboxaq.com/publications/x-wing); hybrid signature spectrums (https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-pquip-hybrid-signature-spectrums/); RFC 4998 (https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4998.html); PQ VRFs (https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/766, https://cic.iacr.org/i/1/3); leanSig (https://pq.ethereum.org/blog/introducing-leansig/).
