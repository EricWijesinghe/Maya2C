Maya2C is built by one developer. Security reports from people outside the
project are how it gets safer, and this page names the people who sent them.

## No cash rewards

**No cash rewards are offered**, and no amount is promised here or anywhere
else. If that ever changes, this page and
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/EricWijesinghe/Maya2C/blob/master/SECURITY.md)
will say so first.

Recognition is what there is for now: a line on this page, a badge, and credit
in the published advisory.

## Who is listed

Nobody is listed yet.

Entries appear here only after the report has been fixed or judged not to be a
problem, and only with the reporter's consent. Nothing is added from a
scanner's output alone.

## How to report

Report privately, never in a public issue:

1. Open
   [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/EricWijesinghe/Maya2C/security/advisories/new).
2. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the commit you tested.
3. Read the response targets and the rules of engagement in
   [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/EricWijesinghe/Maya2C/blob/master/SECURITY.md).

Testing is for your own local devnet or the public testnet only. Do not test
against anyone else's funds or accounts.

## Badge

Reporters of a valid finding can claim the security finder badge on the
[Contributors & badges](/guides/contributors/) page.
