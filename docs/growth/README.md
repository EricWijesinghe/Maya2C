# docs/growth

Designs for how people find, join and stay with Maya2C. Design only: nothing
here is live until the maintainer opens it.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| [ambassador-design.md](ambassador-design.md) | Volunteer ambassadors, non-monetary referrals, developer retention, funnel measurement sources, the legal reasons nothing is paid, and a reviewer checklist. |

Standing rules for anything added here:

- Nothing is paid, in any asset or money. Recognition is the badge system on
  [`/guides/contributors/`](../site/authored/contributors.md) and named credit.
- No price, returns or earnings figures. No "first", "only" or ranking claims.
- Every number names its source; a number without one is written MISSING.
